Niyam (pehle se tay, backtest dekh ke nahi badle)

  1. Trend score: pichhle 20 poore dinon ke log(close) pe seedhi line (OLS regression). t = slope ÷ standard error. t ≥ +2 → sirf BUY, t ≤ −2 → sirf SELL, beech me koi trade nahi.
  2. Asia range: 00:00–07:00 UTC (05:30–12:30 IST) ka high/low. Range daily ATR(14) ke 0.25–1 guna ke beech.
  3. Entry: 07:00–12:00 UTC (12:30–17:30 IST) me pehli H1 candle jo range + 0.05×ATR ke bahar trend ki disha me CLOSE kare → agli candle ke open pe.
  4. SL: range ka beech − buffer. Risk ATR se zyada ho to skip. TP: 2 × risk.
  5. Waqt: 20:00 UTC (01:30 IST) tak na lage to band. Din me ek trade per pair. Har trade pe 1% risk.
  6. Ek candle me SL aur TP dono chhue to SL maana (bura maan ke). Koi martingale/grid nahi.
Zaroori: Backtest bhavishya ki guarantee nahi hai. Live me slippage, spread badhna, news aur broker ka fark nateeje bigaad sakte hain. Kam se kam 2–3 mahine demo pe chalao, phir chhote lot se. Koi bhi system 100% accurate nahi hota. Jo dawa kare, wo ya martingale hai ya jhooth.
